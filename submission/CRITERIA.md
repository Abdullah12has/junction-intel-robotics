# Junction submission requirements

Verified in the signed-in Junction Chrome session on 15 November 2026. This is a submission checklist, not legal advice or a declaration that every performance target has been met.

## Live form

Source: https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/submissions

Existing Intel entry: **Prometheus.** (draft). Preserve the selected Intel Physical AI challenge; there is a separate same-name entry for another challenge.

- Project name: required, at most 30 characters.
- Punchline: required, at most 200 characters.
- Description: required, at most 5,000 characters, Markdown supported.
- Challenge selection: required.
- Optional-looking form fields: demo, source, video and other URL. The general event rules still require source access and a video.
- Presentation upload: PDF, PPTX, PPT, ODP or KEY, maximum 20 MB.
- Save Draft and Submit Final are distinct actions.

## Event rules and timing

Source: https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/guide

- FINAL submission: Sunday 15 November, 10:00 event-local time.
- Pitch video: maximum two minutes.
- Organizers must be able to access the source. The rules state MIT licensing for project submissions; third-party materials retain their own terms.
- Original project code must be written at the event. Do not certify provenance of unreviewed third-party material merely because it is in a source snapshot.
- Peer review: be at the team's registered table by 10:30; five-minute pitch/demo rounds. Participation is required for the main prize.
- Top-five stage pitch: three minutes, followed by three minutes of questions.

## Evidence boundaries for this submission

Simulation footage is labeled. The selected successful rollout uses a MuJoCo expert, not a learned policy. Physical policy-driven motion has been tested, but reliable physical pick-and-place and complete autonomous quality sorting remain unproven. The 32-experiment campaign is a design/launch effort, not a completed benchmark. No measured energy-saving or NPU policy-execution claim is made.
