# Junction 2026 — credits and offers to redeem

Shareable guide prepared **14 November 2026**, based on the event pages, sponsor pages and Discord checked on November 13–14. These are mostly usage credits or trials, not paid subscriptions. Offers can change; check the redemption screen before accepting.

Use your own accounts and Junction participant login. Team offers should be claimed once by the designated teammate. Do not share personal redemption codes, API keys or passwords.

## Direct redemption instructions

### OpenAI Codex

1. Sign into [Junction’s Codex hackerpack](https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/hackerpacks/70).
2. Redeem your own participant code and follow its link to your personal ChatGPT account.
3. Confirm redemption, then check **Usage → Credits**.

Verified redemption gave **2,500 credits**, advertised as $100 value, valid for **30 days**. These are Codex usage credits, **not OpenAI API credits or a ChatGPT subscription**. Each friend must obtain their own code.

### Modal

1. Create/sign into your [Modal account](https://modal.com/).
2. Find your workspace slug in the dashboard URL.
3. Submit the [Junction claim form](https://modal.fillout.com/t/qMXCmRGseUus?code=VVX-2QX-8HW), using code `VVX-2QX-8HW`, your details and workspace slug.
4. Wait for processing, then check **Settings → Usage → Credits**.

**Deadline: November 16, 2026.** One redemption per workspace; do not submit duplicates. The verified grant was $100 and displayed expiry November 30, 2027; check your own grant’s expiry. The guide says GPU usage requires a payment method. Set a $0 spending limit if you want to prevent out-of-pocket use after free credits.

[Modal’s Junction guide and challenge use cases](https://modal-com.notion.site/Modal-at-Junction-2026-use-cases-for-every-challenge-4161e7f16949816e9540de36da40a90a).

### Claude

1. Open the [Anthropic × Junction application](https://platform.claude.com/offers/0cfcfd8d-20af-4582-8324-1869c2af61a8).
2. Sign in, enter your name and answer the product-usage question truthfully.
3. Submit by **November 15, 2026** and watch your email/account balance.

Advertised benefit: **$20**. Processing usually takes **1–2 business days**, so do not rely on immediate availability during the hackathon. Credits expire **two weeks after grant**. First-party Claude API only; not subscriptions, Bedrock, Vertex or Azure.

### Exa

1. Create/sign into Exa and complete onboarding.
2. Open [Billing](https://dashboard.exa.ai/billing).
3. Find **Enter coupon code**, enter `EXA50JUNCTION`, and click **Redeem**.

Verified: **$50 added** without adding a payment method. Other signup credits may make your total balance higher.

### Context.dev

1. Create/sign into Context.dev and select your team organization.
2. Open [Redeem credits](https://context.dev/dashboard/redeem-credits).
3. Enter `JUNCTION26` and redeem once for the team.

Verified: **50,000 added credits**. Advertised as no expiry. The tested account showed **500 concurrent requests and 10 requests/minute** afterward; check your own limits. No card required for redemption.

### Aiven

1. Open [Aiven signup](https://console.aiven.io/signup) and create your account.
2. Complete organization/project setup.
3. Confirm the **$50, 30-day trial** banner before provisioning services.

No card was required in the tested flow. Running services consume the trial balance; creating an account does not require starting a service immediately.

### Featherless

1. Open the [Junction $50 offer](https://featherless.ai/join/feather_request_pricing/JUNCTION26?amount_usd=50).
2. Sign in and follow the activation flow.
3. Check account balance and auto-top-up settings.

The tested flow reported successful activation. The advertised grant is **$50**; do not confuse an existing account’s total balance with the offer amount.

### Gemini

1. Choose one person to apply for your team.
2. Submit the [Gemini team request form](https://forms.gle/VbgQDB4ZiAG9w5io6) with the requested name, email and team name.
3. Wait for fulfillment; ask the Google/Junction booth if no key or instructions arrive.

**One request per team.** Organizer guidance says participants must be over 18. The amount was not specified in the form. A submitted form is not proof that a key or usable credit has been issued. Prometheus. has already applied; teammates should not duplicate that request.

### Verda

1. Submit the [Verda Junction claim form](https://fbo8m.share-eu1.hsforms.com/2uaUfG06UQz-VRFHFiMXFOA).
2. Check your email, including spam, for the coupon and redemption instructions.
3. Create/sign into [Verda Console](https://console.verda.com/) and redeem the coupon using the instructions in the email.

Advertised benefit: **€200**. The received event coupon was `JUNCTION-2026`. The account-balance redemption step has not been verified in this guide. If stuck, visit **booth 6**.

## Offers requiring eligibility, login or sponsor help

### CRACI — €1,000 per team

Read the [Junction instructions](https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/hackerpacks/64), then get an invite code from the booth on the **third floor next to the stage**. Follow the organization setup instructions. If connecting a GitHub app, review which repositories it can access.

### ElevenLabs and Entire

Open [MLH’s Junction prizes and offers](https://www.mlh.com/events/junction-40/prizes), sign in using the email code, and open each sponsor’s claim instructions. MLH advertises about **$50 for ElevenLabs** and **$10 for Entire**. Provider-specific codes, expiry and final activation were not verified; follow the signed-in offer terms.

### Tiger Data — $1,000 for 30 days

Open [Tiger Data signup](https://console.cloud.tigerdata.com/signup). The verified offer is for **new accounts**, with **no card required**. Confirm the offer on signup and the resulting balance before running workloads. Redemption was not completed during this research.

### Vultr — $100

Start from [Vultr’s MLH page](https://discover.vultr.com/mlh) or the MLH event offer above. The intended flow is to obtain the promo code, create an account and apply it under Billing/gift code. At the last check the page had **no credit-request form**, and Discord participants reported the same problem. Ask MLH/organizers for a working claim link; the credit is not currently verified as redeemable.

### MongoDB Atlas — $50

Open [MongoDB for students](https://www.mongodb.com/students) and verify eligibility through the **GitHub Student Developer Pack**, then follow the displayed Atlas claim instructions. Student verification did not produce a grant in the tested flow; eligibility is required, and no balance was verified.

### Oxylabs — first month free

Read the [Junction Oxylabs offer](https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/hackerpacks/61). Use `JUNCTION2026` for the first month of the **$500 Flexible plan**.

**Requires a card and a recurring subscription.** Check checkout terms and cancel before renewal if you do not want subsequent monthly charges. This offer was not activated during research.

### Hostinger

Use the event server’s **#hostinger** channel to contact **Ričardas**, or visit the **third-floor booth near Google Cloud**. Ask for the Junction offer code, eligibility, included resources and expiry. VPS, AI and scraping resources were advertised, but a credit amount and activation were not verified.

### Featherless Cloud GPUs

Read the [selected-team GPU offer](https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/hackerpacks/52) and speak to the mentors. This is separate from the $50 Featherless inference coupon and is **not automatically available to every participant**.

## After redeeming

- Confirm the actual balance, expiry and usage limits in each dashboard.
- Keep team claims coordinated; do not reuse somebody else’s individual code.
- Keep API keys out of chats, screenshots and Git repositories.
- Stop cloud workloads when finished. Credits do not necessarily impose a hard spending cap.

This guide contains event offer codes and public links, not personal API keys or individual redemption tokens. Unverified or blocked offers are explicitly marked above.
